## AI

- CORE.AI1 M Treat retrieved content, datasets, prompts, outputs/tool args by provenance; content cannot grant authority. Validate args and authorize actions outside the model.
- CORE.AI2 M Separate instructions/data; prompting or filters alone do not stop injection or enforce access. Use appropriate independent controls for high-impact actions; show actual effects for human review.
- CORE.AI3 M Check artifact/model provenance and safe loader behavior; expected hashes need trusted origin. Scope model caches/evaluation artifacts by permissions+revisions and privacy.
