## I18N

- **BE.I18N1 M** Keep absolute instants, local dates, wall-clock times, and time zones as distinct domain concepts. Specify storage/serialization rules for each.
- **BE.I18N2 M** Use current time-zone databases and appropriate locale/calendar libraries; test DST gaps/overlaps, leap years, end-of-month, and recurring schedule semantics where applicable.
- **BE.I18N3 M** Do not infer business event order from wall-clock timestamps across distributed hosts; use authoritative transaction/version ordering or causal identifiers when needed.
- **BE.I18N4 M** Preserve valid Unicode and domain-specific normalization; avoid globally stripping accents, confusables, or punctuation from legal names, addresses, and identifiers without a defined policy.
- **BE.I18N5 M** Specify locale-aware parsing/formatting for decimal, currency, dates, units, and text, while keeping canonical internal representations stable across boundaries.
- **BE.I18N6 S** Prefer locale-neutral domain storage with localized presentation; localizing at the data layer is appropriate only for genuinely locale-specific content or business law.
- **BE.I18N7 M** Avoid float/time conversion and inconsistent rounding across services for billing, tax, interest, quantities, and reconciliation; define authoritative units and precision.
- **BE.I18N8 S** Test sorting, casefolding, Unicode normalization, collations, and unique constraints for supported writing systems rather than assuming ASCII or one database locale.
- **BE.I18N9 M** For scheduled jobs and legal deadlines, distinguish UTC elapsed-time intervals from local business-calendar scheduling; define DST retry/missed-run behavior.
